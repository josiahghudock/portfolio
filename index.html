Instruments & Technical Experience

My primary instruments are drums and guitar. I specialize in the prog rock/metal style and have an affinity for polymetric compositions and odd groupings/tuplets.

I also have practical skills and experience in recording and mixing, as well as composing and transcribing for small ensembles.

Experience

Percussion

I have performed live with various rock, metal, jazz, afro-cuban, and classical ensembles, and have recorded drums for Pittsburgh rock bands Constellation X and 446. I like to combine both highly dynamic and improvisational performance with complex rhythmic constructions. I have also developed techniques for xylophone, timpani, cymbals, chimes, and other percussion instruments.

Guitar

I have performed electric guitar in rock and jazz settings as well as solo classical guitar. Most of my composition is done on guitar. Lately I have been working on using live-looping to cover pop tunes and jazz standards in solo performance.
