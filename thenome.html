About The 'Nome

The 'Nome is a free user-friendly online metronome with advanced capabilities. It is built specifically for practicing odd-time, polyrhythmic, odd-tuplet, or nested tuplet rhythms. Ultra-Nerd mode allows for "irrational" time signatures and extremely precise sequencing. If you found this tool useful, have questions, or suggestions please let me know!
